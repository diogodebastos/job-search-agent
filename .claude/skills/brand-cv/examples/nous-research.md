# Nous Research: visual language (studied 2026-11-18, nousresearch.com)
- Colour: accent #0000F2, ink #000091, paper #F2F2F2. Two-colour system; white only inside art.
- Display: Oswald Light 300, uppercase, enormous (a title spans the page). Open licence: used.
- Labels: Mona Sans Condensed 400-500 uppercase, tracked; kickers in Mona Sans Extended 700 at 9px.
- Body: Aeonik Fono (paid trial) and Mona Sans 13px. Substitute: Mona Sans throughout.
- Layout: centred hero, thin blue frame round the viewport, hairline dividers, 1mm radius, lots of air, blue field at the foot.
- Imagery: 1-bit ordered dither, blue and white, anime eyes, CRT monitors, palms, four-point sparkles, data rain.
- Signatures: small outlined cards with a glyph (nav centre, footer trio); square bullets.
- Mapping: name as the hero title; contact line as the nav; a personal card in the centre; dithered banner under the name; three cards in the footer; art field closing page 2.
- Not used: their logo card, their wing mark, their hero art, "The internet's own AI".
