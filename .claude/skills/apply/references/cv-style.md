# Tailored CV style

## Shape
- At most 2 A4 pages; one page is fine for under about 8 years of experience.
- Order: name and contact line, Summary, Experience, (Projects or Portfolio if relevant), Skills, Education, Languages. Standard heading names: résumé software looks for them.
- One column. No tables, text boxes, icons, photos, skill bars or emoji.
- Dates as month and year ("March 2022 – Present"), newest first, same format everywhere.
- Contact line as plain text: city and country, email, phone, LinkedIn, portfolio.

## Length plan (about 80 lines for two pages at 10pt)
- Header and contact: 3 lines. Summary: 4 to 5.
- Current role: 5 to 7 bullets, about 20 lines. Previous role: 3 to 5. Older roles: 1 to 2, or one line each under "Earlier".
- Skills: 4 to 5 grouped lines, languages included. Education: 2 to 3 lines.
- If it runs over by a few lines, shorten bullets that wrap by one or two words. Don't shrink the font.

## Writing
- Bullet shape: **verb + what + how + result**. "Raised Black Friday return on ad spend from 3.9 to 5.2 by moving budget from prospecting to retargeting, measured in GA4."
- A result goes in only if it is in `facts.md`. Qualitative results are fine ("now the report the category team plans from").
- Put the posting's must-have words in the summary and the first bullets of the current role, where a fact supports them. A reader skims the top third of page one.
- Use the posting's exact names for tools and methods, when they are the same thing the user has used.
- First person without "I" in bullets; "I" is fine in the summary.
- Cut: "responsible for", "various", "etc.", "passionate", "dynamic", "results-driven", "team player", "detail-oriented".
- Reorder bullets and roles' emphasis for each job; never change a fact to fit.

## Per kind of role (what to lead with)
- **Performance and digital marketing:** channels run hands-on, budget, the best result with its measurement, tools.
- **Brand and content:** campaigns owned, audience and reach, the idea, who you briefed, a portfolio link.
- **CRM and email:** list size, flows built, open, click and revenue numbers, the platform.
- **Management roles:** team size and what you decided, then the results the team delivered.
- **Agency side:** number and type of clients, retention or growth, biggest account.
- **Anything else:** what the posting asks for first, matched with your strongest proof of it.
