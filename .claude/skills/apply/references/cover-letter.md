# Cover letter

Résumé software reads the CV. The letter is for the person who opens the file, so write to a person.

## Rules
- 250 to 350 words, one page. No emoji. The user's own plain voice, first person.
- Every claim comes from `facts.md`. Don't repeat the CV; tell the story behind one or two lines of it.
- **One honest gap line**, with what they are doing about it. It builds more trust than it costs.
- Never name confidential clients. Never flatter the company with things anyone could say.
- Same language as the posting. Address a named person if the posting gives one.

## Structure
1. **Opening (2 to 3 sentences).** Something specific: a problem they noticed and fixed, a result with its story, or a real connection to the company's product or a line in the posting. Not "I am writing to apply for".
2. **Why this role (3 to 5 sentences).** Two or three of the posting's must-haves, each matched to evidence.
3. **The gap, honestly (1 to 2 sentences).**
4. **Close (2 sentences).** Where they are based, languages if relevant, and the ask: a conversation.
5. Sign-off and name.

## File shape
```markdown
# Full Name

City, Country | {{EMAIL}} | {{PHONE}}

12 January 2026 · **Re: Job title (reference, if any)**

Dear <name or team>,

...

Best regards,\
Full Name
```

## Check
- One page after rendering. Read it aloud in your head: if a sentence sounds like a template, rewrite it.
- List each claim's fact ID in the truth audit.
