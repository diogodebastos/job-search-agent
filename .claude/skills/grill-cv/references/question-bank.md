# Question bank

Pick the questions that fit; don't march through the list. One per message.

## For every role
- In one sentence, what was the job there to achieve?
- What did a normal week look like? Which part took most of your time?
- What did you personally decide, and what needed someone's sign-off?
- How big was it? (team, budget, customers, accounts, volume, markets, stores, users)
- What changed because you were there? What was different when you left, or is different now?
- What are you proudest of from that job? Why that one?
- What was the hardest problem, and what did you do about it?
- How was your work measured? Which number did your manager care about?
- Did you train, onboard or lead anyone, formally or informally?
- Did you start anything that is still running?
- What tools did you use every day? Which could you teach to a colleague?
- Why did you move on?

## To turn a vague line into a fact
- "Managed X": how many, how much, how often, and what did managing involve?
- "Improved X": from what to what, over what period, measured how?
- "Worked with stakeholders": who, and what did you need from each other?
- "Supported the launch of": which part was yours?
- "Experience in": when did you last do it, and on what?

## To test a number
- Where does that number come from? (a report, a dashboard, memory)
- Is it yours alone or the team's?
- Compared with what? (last year, a target, a control group, the market)
- If an interviewer asked "how do you know it was your work that caused it?", what would you say?

## By profession

**Marketing (digital, brand, performance, content, CRM)**
- Which channels did you run yourself? (paid social, search, email, SEO, influencers, affiliates, events, PR)
- What budget did you handle, per month or per campaign? Who decided the split?
- Pick your best campaign: what was the goal, the audience, the idea, the spend and the result? (reach, CTR, CPA, ROAS, conversion rate, revenue, leads, sign-ups, followers, open rate)
- How was it attributed, and what was the comparison?
- Did you run tests? (creative, audience, landing page) What did one teach you?
- Which platforms and tools hands-on? (Meta Ads, Google Ads, GA4, Looker Studio, a CRM or email tool, a CMS, a social scheduler, Canva/Figma)
- Did you brief or manage agencies, freelancers or creators? How many?
- B2C or B2B? Which sectors? Any e-commerce, retail media or marketplace work?
- Did you own a calendar, a brand guideline, a report that others relied on?

**Sales and account management**
- Quota and attainment, deal size, cycle length, portfolio size, new business versus renewals, biggest win, how leads reached you.

**Engineering and data**
- What you built, for how many users, in which stack, what it replaced, how it's run and monitored, what broke and how you fixed it, what is public.

**Design and creative**
- The brief, your role, the constraints, what shipped, how success was judged, and a link.

**Operations, finance, HR, customer service**
- Volumes handled, time or cost saved, error rates, processes you changed, systems used, audits passed, people served.

**Students and career changers**
- Projects, coursework with a real output, volunteering, part-time jobs (they prove reliability), anything you organised, anything you taught yourself and can show.

## Closing questions
- Is there anything you do well that this CV still doesn't show?
- Is there anything here you would be nervous to be asked about? (Fix or remove it.)
